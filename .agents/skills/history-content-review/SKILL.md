# History Content Review

1. Read `docs/HISTORY_CONTENT_STANDARD.md` and the relevant product specification.
2. Extract every checkable historical claim from the proposed content.
3. Classify each as fact, interpretation, disputed/uncertain, tradition/legend, or reconstruction/fiction.
4. Verify important claims against the strongest practical sources available.
5. Check non-text details too: chronology, geography, clothing, objects, architecture, institutions, language, and visual prompts.
6. When sources disagree, preserve the disagreement and avoid false certainty.
7. Adapt wording to the learner only after the historical meaning is stable.
8. Keep source metadata separate from learner-facing copy.
9. Before completion, run the checklist in `docs/HISTORY_CONTENT_STANDARD.md`.

Do not treat AI-generated text as a source.
